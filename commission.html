HAND-PAINTED PET PORTRAITS

Custom oil pet portraits from your photos, delivered to Australia

Commission an original oil painting of your dog, cat or other beloved companion. Each portrait is painted by hand in our Ho Chi Minh City studio using the reference photographs you provide. Choose your canvas size and number of pets above to see the estimated price in Australian dollars before proceeding.

What photos should I send?

Choose clear, well-lit photographs that show your pet’s face, markings and expression. You can upload your reference photos during checkout. If you are unsure which image will work best, ask the studio before ordering.

How do I order a pet portrait in Australia?

Select your canvas size, number of pets and delivery destination, then proceed to checkout to upload photos and provide your address. You can choose a 50% deposit or full payment at the payment step.

Are these paintings printed or digitally generated?

No. Your commissioned portrait is an original oil painting made by hand. Explore real examples of our artwork and read our guide to choosing the best pet photo and our painting process.

What about international delivery?

Australia, the United States, the United Kingdom and Canada are available destinations in the order form. Standard shipping is included for eligible orders; customs charges may apply. Please read our shipping and commission policies before paying.

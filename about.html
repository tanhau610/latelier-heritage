Our philosophy

More than a likeness.

Our studio focuses on original oil pet portraits created from photographs. Every painting is approached as an individual artwork, with attention to the expression, colouring and character of the animal portrayed.

Working with a portrait artist in Vietnam, we prepare commissions for pet lovers in Australia and other supported international destinations. We believe honest craftsmanship and thoughtful communication matter as much as the finished painting.

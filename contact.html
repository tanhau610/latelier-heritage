A few helpful answers

Good to know.

How do I begin a portrait commission?

Visit Commission, select your preferred dimensions and number of pets, then choose reference photos. The studio will confirm your request before requesting payment.

Can I send more than one photograph?

Yes. The commission form currently supports up to five reference images for the studio to review. Uploading is preview-only until the live ordering system is connected.

Is standard shipping included?

Prices are planned to include standard shipping to eligible destinations. Any import taxes or delivery exceptions will be clarified before payment. Shipping coverage must be confirmed before the public launch.

Can I request something different?

Yes. Describe your idea in the commission form or use the general enquiry form. The studio will assess what is possible before confirming a quote.

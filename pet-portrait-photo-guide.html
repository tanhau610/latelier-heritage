THE PORTRAIT GUIDE

Which photo makes
the best pet portrait?

A helpful guide to choosing a reference photograph for an original oil painting of your dog or cat.

START WITH THEIR CHARACTER

Choose the photo that feels most like them.

The best reference is not always the most professional photograph. A clear, familiar expression often matters more than a perfectly posed image. Our artists use your photos as references for an original, hand-painted oil portrait, not a printed reproduction.

See original pet portrait artwork

Five things to look for in your reference photo

  1. Clear eyes and face. Choose a photograph where the eyes, nose and distinctive markings are visible. Avoid heavy blur and strong filters.
  2. Soft, natural light. Window light or open shade often reveals fur colours and details better than harsh flash or deep shadows.
  3. A familiar expression. Look for the head tilt, alert ears or gentle gaze that makes your pet recognisable to you.
  4. A useful angle. Eye-level photographs tend to show the face clearly. If you love a different angle, send it too.
  5. Original image quality. Where possible, upload the original photo rather than a compressed screenshot.

Can you combine two pets in one painting?

Yes. Choose the number of pets when you start your commission. If your pets were photographed separately, share clear images of each and describe the composition you have in mind. We can review whether the references are suitable before painting begins.

What if my favourite photo is old or blurry?

Please send the favourite image alongside any clearer photos you have. A second photograph can help us understand coat colours and distinctive markings. We cannot promise that every detail can be reconstructed from a heavily blurred or damaged image, so we will discuss any limitations before proceeding.

How many photos should I upload?

Our online order form accepts 1–5 reference photographs. Start with the image you love most and add a few useful alternatives showing the face, coat and markings. You do not need to upload five photos if one or two are already clear.

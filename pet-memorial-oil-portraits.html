Some people choose a portrait to celebrate a pet who is still beside them. Others commission one after saying goodbye. In either case, we begin with the details that made your companion unmistakably yours: the expression, the markings and the little tilt of their head.

Each ATELIER HAU portrait is painted by hand in oils from your reference photographs, not produced as a digital print.

Can a memorial portrait be painted from an older photograph?

Often, yes. A clear photograph showing your pet's face is the most useful starting point, but it does not need to have been taken professionally. If you have several older images, share the ones that best show their expression and markings. We will discuss any limitations before proceeding; details that are not visible cannot always be reconstructed accurately.

Our reference photo guide explains how to choose and prepare images.

Choosing the right portrait

A close-up portrait can bring attention to the eyes and expression. A wider composition may include more of the body or a favourite setting, depending on the photos available and the selected canvas size. If you are unsure, contact our studio before ordering.

See sizes and prices in Australian dollars, then select your preferred canvas size and number of pets in the commission form.

How the commission works

  1. Choose. Select a size and see your price in AUD.
  2. Share. Upload your reference photos and delivery details.
  3. Commission. Choose a 50% deposit or full payment. Our studio reviews the photographs and discusses any issues before painting.
  4. Receive. Your original painting is prepared for international delivery in accordance with our shipping and commission policies.

Painted in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, with orders welcomed from Australia and other supported destinations. Delivery dates are not guaranteed at checkout.

A portrait, without pressure

There is no right moment to commission a remembrance portrait. If you would like to ask about a photograph, a particular expression or a gift for someone who has lost a pet, you can write to us first. We will help you understand what can be painted from the references you have.

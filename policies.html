Studio information

Policies & privacy.

ATELIER HAU is based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. For questions about a commission, email hello@atelierhau.com.

Please review the information below before placing a commission. Contact the studio if any details of your order or delivery need clarification.

Pricing and shipping

Prices are shown in Australian dollars (AUD). The checkout price includes standard shipping for supported destinations: Australia, the United States, the United Kingdom and Canada. Import duties, local taxes and customs fees are not included and may be payable by the recipient. Remote locations, special packaging or unusual requests may require a separate quote; if the displayed checkout terms cannot be honoured, we will contact you to discuss options rather than add charges without your agreement.

Commission approval and payment

A contact enquiry is not a paid order. For orders placed through the online checkout, you may choose to pay a 50% deposit or the full amount through PayPal. Payment may be taken before our studio has reviewed your reference photographs. We will review your photos and contact you if composition, timing or other details need clarification. If you choose the deposit, the remaining balance is requested before dispatch. Review the amount and payment stage shown at checkout before authorising payment.

Original artwork and delivery

Each painting is created individually. Painting, drying, packaging and international delivery times vary by order and are not guaranteed at checkout. We will discuss an estimated schedule after reviewing your order. Oil paintings require appropriate drying and protective packaging before dispatch; we will confirm the packaging and shipping arrangements for your destination.

Reference images and privacy

Reference photos submitted for a commission should be used solely to assess and fulfil that commission, with restricted access and defined retention rules. Portrait enquiry photographs and order details are stored privately in Cloudflare R2 and are accessible to authorised studio staff through a protected dashboard. To ask about access to or deletion of information submitted to the studio, please use the contact form. Certain transaction records may need to be retained for legal or accounting purposes.

Changes, cancellations and returns

Because portraits are made to order, please contact the studio promptly if you need to change or cancel a commission. The studio will review the production stage and applicable consumer-law rights before confirming any available remedy. Nothing here limits rights that cannot legally be excluded.
